Works free in your browser

  • Reading the song and splitting it into scenes by loudness changes
  • Editing scene timing, scene notes, and captions
  • Preview with fades, plus slow pan and zoom on art you upload
  • Recording the preview to a video file

Needs an outside service

  • AI-drawn scenes or animation (an image or video generation API)
  • True lyric timing (a speech-to-text or alignment service)
  • Fast rendering or guaranteed MP4 on every phone (a server, or ffmpeg compiled for the browser)

Limits to know

  • Export records in real time: a 3-minute song takes 3 minutes. Keep Chrome open and the screen on.
  • You get MP4 only if your browser can record it. Otherwise you get WebM.
  • Saving may be blocked in the hosted preview. Use your own hosted copy, or the Share button.
  • Phone memory is limited. Long songs at High quality can fail, so use Standard or Draft and run the 3-second test first.
  • Browsers write WebM without a duration header, so some players cannot seek. It plays fine in Chrome.

Host it from your Android phone

  1. In Chrome, sign in at github.com and create a repository named animusic (public).
  2. Tap Add file, then Upload files, and pick this file. Name it index.html.
  3. Tap Commit changes.
  4. Open Settings, then Pages. Under Branch pick main, and Save.
  5. After a minute your app is live at yourname.github.io/animusic. In Chrome’s menu, tap Add to Home screen.

Automatic lyric alignment

The aligner server contract: POST multipart with audio (file), language, and lines (JSON list of {text, norm}). Reply JSON {“words”:[{“w”,“s”,“e”,“q”}]}, one entry per word of each line’s norm text in order, or {“lines”:[{“s”,“e”}]}, one per line. Add “error” to explain a refusal. “q” is shown only if your server sends it. Without a server, use Tap to sync.
